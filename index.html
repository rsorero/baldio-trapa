The Antarr proposal for the Trapa commons

Antarr proposes to take over 365 ha of the Santa Cruz da Trapa baldio for 80 years, at €7,500 a year, and to replant it under its own forest plan. This binder treats that proposal as what the commoners (compartes) are being asked to approve. No evidence was found that a cessão has already been voted or signed.

Key findings

  • The real offer. Antarr's proposal of 13 Aug 2026 was prepared by Sonae Arauco, its operational partner and a wood-panel maker. It offers 80 years at €7,500 a year, rising by the official rent-update factor, plus €5,000 for the community's existing young pine. That's about 5% of Antarr's stated €14.7M investment.
  • Unclear what's being voted. The October notice names only "the forest plan for the ceded area". Antarr's own timetable planned cessão votes and a contract signature for Aug–Sep 2026, but no notices or minutes of such assemblies were found.
  • What would be planted:
    • 104 ha of conifers: maritime pine 88 ha, plus 16 ha of non-native Monterey pine and Douglas fir
    • cork oak 44 ha, mixed broadleaves 41.5 ha, oaks 13 ha, chestnut 4.5 ha
    • riverside woodland kept at 32 ha
    • the 6 ha of eucalyptus removed; scrub cut from 163 ha to 71 ha
  • The site, per the proposal: 100% forest regime, REN, and Natura 2000 (Serras da Freita e Arada) with wolf and otter. It contains the Cabeço da Moura fortified settlement and 8 turbines of the Arada–Montemuro wind farm. It burns at 6.7% a year against 4.0% for the municipality; 37% has burned three or more times, most recently in 2017.
  • Promised in writing: the forest plan "will always have to be approved by the commoners". The proposal also lists coordinated use for beekeeping, hunting, firewood, fruit, leisure and tourism, but not grazing.
  • Who Antarr is: a forestry investor funded 50/50 by the Gulbenkian Foundation and Efanor (the Azevedo/Sonae holding). It reports 10,000 ha in 24 management units. Its Riodouro baldio contract runs 40 years.
  • Where it is: the proposal's 509 ha study area, placed approximately from its own maps, lies north of Santa Cruz da Trapa and shares a 1.4 km edge with Antarr's 520 ha at Carvalhais. Together they would form one block of about 1,075 ha. About 197 ha of the Trapa area falls in the Carvalhais e Candal parish.
  • Câmara: its 2026 agendas make no mention of Antarr or a cessão.

  • The itemised investment adds up to €3.93M against a €14.7M headline.
    • 
      
  • The rent projection assumes ≈0.5% growth a year, below the index the proposal itself cites.

Lei n.º 75/2017 (Lei dos Baldios) and related rules

    
What Antarr may do with the land

    
A cessão de exploração gives Antarr the right to work the land commercially, within limits. Most protections cover ownership. Protecting the land's condition depends on what the contract and the plan say. This is not legal advice; confirm the details with Baladi or a lawyer.

  


  

    
What the law sets

    

      
TopicRuleArticle

      
        
PurposeTemporary, paid right to exploit the economic potential of the land: agriculture, livestock, forestry, hunting.36.º n.º 2–3

        
OwnershipBaldios are outside legal commerce. They can't be sold, taken over, claimed by long use (usucapião), mortgaged or pledged.6.º

        
Scope"Limited parts" of the baldio.36.º n.º 2

        
ApprovalTwo-thirds of those present, both for the cessão and for the land-use plan.24.º n.º 2; 36.º n.º 4

        
DurationUp to 20 years, renewable automatically in 20-year blocks up to 80. After that, a new express vote is needed.36.º n.º 5–6

        
Contract must stateParties, land registration, map, area, equipment, price and updates, payment dates and method, term, and a copy of the approving minutes.36.º n.º 7

        
Traditional usesThird-party use must respect traditional uses by commoners.10.º

        
WaterCommoners can't be deprived of springs or groundwater, and keep the flow the baldio needs.15.º

        
RevenueNot distributable to individuals. Must go to management, at least 20% of any surplus into reserves, social and cultural benefit, or collective ends. Forest surplus is reinvested in forestry.14.º

      
    

    
Outside the baldio law, new planting needs ICNF approval within the regional forest plan (PROF Centro Litoral). New eucalyptus is restricted. Natura 2000 areas need ICNF's environmental opinion. Taking water needs an APA licence. Stream margins are a public easement.

  


  

    
Where the law leaves the land exposed

    
    
      
  • No standard for the land's condition at handback. The baldio law sets none, so it has to be in the contract.
    • 
      
  • Renewal can be automatic "taking into account the amortisation of the investment". Once signed, the community may only get out on proof of breach.
    • 
      
  • The plan is short, the contract long. Forest plans run 10–15 years. Later revisions may not return to the assembly unless the contract says so.
    • 
      
  • The operator could change. Transfer to another company normally needs consent, but the contract can give it in advance.
    • 
      
  • Pine is production forestry. Clear-fell cycles of 25–45 years mean up to two cycles in 70 years.
    • 
      
  • Carbon schemes can lock land use for decades beyond a contract.
    • 
      
  • Water can be affected indirectly. Dense pine reduces spring flow, and ground preparation and herbicides affect water quality. Antarr's riverside restoration (10% at Carvalhais) helps.
    • 
    

  


  

    
The proposal against the law

    

      
ProposalLawAssessment

      
        
80 years, renegotiable at 4020-year terms, renewable up to 80; a new contract after that needs an express vote (36.º n.º 5–6)Legal, but the maximum. Ask for 20 years, with each renewal by a new two-thirds vote.

        
€7,500/yr, updated by the official rent factorContract must state price, updates, payment dates and method (36.º n.º 7 f–h)Get the update rule, a floor, and the payment account in writing.

        
"Plan always approved by the commoners"The assembly approves land-use plans by two-thirds (24.º n.º 1 f, n.º 2)Consistent. Make it a contract clause covering revisions too.

        
Coordinated use for beekeeping, hunting, leisure, tourism, firewood and fruitThird-party use must respect traditional uses (10.º)Grazing is missing, and "coordinated" is undefined.

        
Watercourses enhancedCommoners can't be deprived of springs or groundwater (15.º)Add an explicit "no water rights ceded" clause.

Clauses to insist on

    
    
      
  1. No automatic renewal. Each 20-year extension needs a new two-thirds vote.
    2. 
      
  2. Plan revisions return to the assembly for approval.
    3. 
      
  3. No eucalyptus, a cap on non-native species, a minimum share of native broadleaves, and conservation areas fixed on a map.
    4. 
      
  4. Limits on clear-fell size and timing.
    5. 
      
  5. An annual report to the assembly and audit committee. FSC certification as a contractual obligation.
    6. 
      
  6. Termination for breach, a defined condition for the returned land, and a restoration bond (caução).
    7. 
      
  7. No transfer to another company without assembly approval.
    8. 
      
  8. Carbon and biodiversity credits owned by or shared with the community, and no long carbon commitments without its consent.
    9. 
      
  9. No water rights ceded. Springs, tanks and levadas mapped and protected. New water points stay with the community and are open to firefighters.
    10. 
      
  10. Grazing, hives, firewood, hunting and access written in, with maps.
    11. 
      
  11. Wind-farm rents explicitly excluded from the cessão and kept by the baldio.
    12. 
      
  12. An independent valuation of the existing young pine before it is handed over.
    13. 
      
  13. Protection of the Cabeço da Moura archaeological site, and of the Natura 2000 habitats.
